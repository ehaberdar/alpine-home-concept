Atelier

A small family business, two countries, one workshop.

In Belp

We are based in Belp, near Bern. From here we choose the models, answer every message and organise delivery. When you write to us, you talk to the people who run the brand.

In İnegöl

Our furniture is made in a family cabinetmaking workshop in İnegöl, near Bursa, Turkey's furniture capital. The sculptural pieces are cut layer by layer, then assembled and finished by hand.

In between

Every piece is checked before packing, protected with custom padding and sent by express service. We follow the order to your door.
